Marketing starts with listening.

I plan, create and run marketing for all kinds of businesses: strategy, content, paid ads, audience research and training. I don’t stick to one platform or one industry. I pick what fits the customer and the goal. I also play the saxophone, and it taught me the same habit: listen first, then play.

How I work

Three steps, every time.

01

Listen

I start with your customers, your market and your goal. No money moves before that.

02

Then a simple plan: who we talk to, what we say, where, and what it costs.

03

Create

I make the content, run the campaigns, check the results and fix what isn’t working.

Fitness & wellness

Six trainers, lots of testing

I helped six trainers work out which offers and audiences actually bring customers. A chat cost anywhere from under EGP 2 to over EGP 50, so the testing mattered more than the budget.

I tried discount offers, 3-month plans, Ramadan and Eid campaigns, posters, carousels and videos, lookalike audiences, and Facebook against Instagram.

About EGP 23K spent, 1,171 chats started and 56K interactions on posts, between Nov 2024 and Nov 2026.

Away from work

I play the saxophone.

Listening

A player hears the room before the first note. I try to hear the customer before the first ad.

Structure and room to improvise

Music has a structure and some freedom inside it. A campaign works the same way: a plan first, then quick tests and changes.

Timing

Practice

Skill comes from repeating things. Testing and tuning campaigns is the same habit.
