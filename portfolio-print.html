Marketing starts with listening.

Good marketing doesn’t start with a playbook. It starts with your customers — what they want, what they scroll past, what makes them buy. Listen to that and the strategy writes itself. It’s the same rule the saxophone teaches: listen first, then play.

Real Estate Apartments and Lands

People looking for a flat or a plot of land messaged the seller directly. Each conversation cost about 2 EGP.

Goal: A property seller needed real buyer conversations for apartments in Faisal and land in Mansoura–Talkha, not just ad views.

Work: I ran message campaigns and engagement campaigns, tried photos against video, and compared people in Mansoura with people across Egypt.

Result: Buyers messaged for about 2 EGP a chat. The best campaign started 644 conversations for 1,392 EGP. All four properties sold: 760,000 EGP in commission on 30.8M EGP of apartments and land.

Five trainers, one retreat, one question

Five trainers, a retreat in Aswan, one question: what actually makes someone start a chat? We tested offers, hooks and audiences until the numbers talked - chats from under 2 to over 50 EGP and a playbook that kept working.

Goal: Five fitness trainers and a yoga retreat needed to know which offers and audiences actually bring paying clients.

Work: I tried discount offers, 3-month plans, Ramadan and Eid campaigns, posters, carousels and videos, lookalike audiences, and Facebook against Instagram.

Result: About 23.1K EGP spent turned into 1,173 chats and 55K post interactions between Nov 2024 and Nov 2026, plus a clear playbook for each trainer. Six campaigns have sales attached: 162,000 EGP in subscriptions and retreat spots.

A café, a mental health platform and an NGO

Three very different brands, all posting on a schedule with Arabic captions. For the café I also ran campaigns to get people through the door.

Goal: Three brands needed steady, on-brand content every week, and the café also wanted people through the door.

Work: I planned the content calendars (reels, carousels, photo sets and YouTube videos) for Kapri Cafe, the Medium mental health platform and the Al-Noon Foundation for Family Care.

Result: Kapri's December campaigns brought 67 and 90 chats at 2.46 EGP and 6.01 EGP each, and the café reports its income grew about 30% that month. Al-Noon published 18 posts on 5 platforms in two months.

Nefr Story

An ancient-Egypt-inspired clothing brand. On a small budget, one ad brought about 1,500 clicks for under 710 EGP.

Goal: Launch an ancient-Egypt-inspired clothing brand from zero, on almost no budget.

Work: I wrote the launch copy, designed the pieces (each one carries a QR code that opens its story), and ran video, photo and discount ads.

Result: One ad reached 32,621 people and brought 1,516 clicks for about 710 EGP, or 0.47 EGP a click.
